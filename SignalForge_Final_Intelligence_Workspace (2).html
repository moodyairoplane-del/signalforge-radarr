The evidence workflow

Designed to make research auditable, not mysterious.

4 STEPS
01 / DISCOVERFind a trigger

Search public company pages, job posts, launches, and announcements.

02 / VERIFYOpen the source

Capture the exact URL, source date, access date, and relevant excerpt.

03 / QUALIFYExplain the fit

State why the event may matter and what still needs confirmation.

04 / ACTSuggest next step

Draft a respectful, specific outreach angle tied to the evidence.
