Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This website is currently a demo/concept interface. This policy describes the visible demo's intended data handling; it is not a claim that production analytics, account, or contact systems are configured.

Information you provide

The current demo does not provide an external contact submission or account-registration service. The research-brief dialog lets you create text locally and copy it to your clipboard; it does not intentionally submit that brief to SignalForge Radar.

Demo and sample data

The prospect records shown on the homepage are illustrative examples. They are not presented as live monitoring results or verified claims about actual businesses. Do not rely on them for business decisions.

Cookies, analytics, and third parties

This page does not document any configured analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking service. If such services are added, this policy should be updated to identify them and explain their purpose and relevant choices.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted through a web deployment platform. The hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as request metadata or security logs, under its own policies. The site owner should confirm the active provider settings and update this section if additional data collection is enabled.

Data retention and security

The demo does not describe a server-side user database or a data-submission workflow. Any future account, contact, or research storage feature should explain what is stored, why it is needed, how long it is retained, and how it is protected.

Your choices and questions

Please avoid entering confidential, sensitive, or personal information into the demo. A verified contact address has not yet been configured. Before production use, the site owner should add a working privacy contact and confirm the policy against the actual hosting, analytics, and data-processing configuration.

Policy updates

This policy may be revised when the website's functionality or data practices change. The update date above should be changed when material revisions are made.
